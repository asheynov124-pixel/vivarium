## PRODUCER

**Eyebrow:** Light and vent  
**Example strain:** *Kormie*  
**Body-plan:** Sessile lobe. Seven-point outline, almost no heading. Stays put unless the pool moves out from under it.

A body that eats the map. Photosynthesis above .42, faster traits asleep. Light preference splits the house — high prefers the Sun pool and the Glade; low sits on Vent and Silt and waits for dark.

- **Season:** High sun is a feast. Dark season is the exam. Stored reserve, or a taste for vents, is what copies.
- **Death:** Crowded out (soft shade from neighbors), drought, grazed to a stump, old.
- **The dish keeps:** whoever still covers a patch when the year turns.

## GRAZER

**Eyebrow:** Eating producers  
**Example strain:** *Vekcai*, splits like *Brackum*  
**Body-plan:** Elongated ellipse, eye forward. Moves.

A mouth aimed at the green. Grazing high, photo low. It has to find a producer inside sense range and bite. Armor on the producer, attack on the grazer, size on both — that's the whole fight.

- **Season:** Follows the flush. When High sun fattens the producers, grazers bloom a season late. When the pool fades they starve on empty ground or learn to scavenge.
- **Death:** Starvation between patches, hunters, senescence.
- **The dish keeps:** a line that can cross a glade without running out.

## HUNTER

**Eyebrow:** Eating movers  
**Example strain:** *Nimon*  
**Body-plan:** Long ellipse, high speed, pupil on the selected one. Boldness decides whether it closes or breaks off.

Carnivory on, photo off, target smaller than itself and not a producer. Bite is costly and the cooldown is real. Too many hunters and the grazers vanish; then the hunters go with them.

- **Season:** Dark does not feed it. It lives on whatever still runs. A strike that kills the herd is a feast for one tick and a famine after.
- **Death:** Empty sense-range, bigger hunters, age.
- **The dish keeps:** whoever can find a mover after the thinning.

## SCAVENGER

**Eyebrow:** Eating the dead  
**Example strain:** *Thaeorar*  
**Body-plan:** Mover ellipse, often darker, camo toward silt.

The dish already turns every corpse into a mote — energy ≈ 28% of the dead body's tank, drifting south. Scavengers are the ones who read that rain. Strike is their weather: "a clearing — and a feast."

- **Season:** Dark season and thinnings are their High sun. In bright years they look like a mistake.
- **Death:** Motes sink and fade. A clean year with no deaths starves them.
- **The dish keeps:** the cleanup crew that can wait.

## PARASITE

**Eyebrow:** Siphoning a living host  
**Example strain:** *Lumpros*  
**Body-plan:** Flattened ellipse, latched. Intent reads Latched.

Small, parasite gene above .62. It does not kill the way a hunter does — it subtracts hp and energy every tick and holds on. Host must be larger. Cooperation does not reach it.

- **Season:** Hosts that survive Dark carry them through. A host that dies in a thinning drops them into the silt with everyone else.
- **Death:** Host gone, no second latch in range, age (short lifespan in the seed).
- **The dish keeps:** a line that can find another body before the first one ends.

## COLONY

**Eyebrow:** Sharing with close kin  
**Example strain:** the first line that crosses coop > .72 without a strong trophic trait  
**Body-plan:** Same lumpy producer-like outline, drawn in a knot. Intent Clustering.

Not a sixth trophic level. A social setting on top of a weak mouth. Energy passes to poorer neighbors inside 32px if gene-distance < .09. Kin threads are this card made visible.

- **Season:** High sun lets a knot sit still. Dark tests whether sharing is cheaper than moving.
- **Death:** A parasite on the knot, or a hunter that treats the cluster as one meal.
- **The dish keeps:** the knot that does not hoard.
